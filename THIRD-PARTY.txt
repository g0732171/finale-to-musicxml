Runtime license notices are retained in runtime/.

musx2mxl 0.2.9, MIT
https://github.com/joris-vaneyghen/musx2mxl

finale-file-parser 0.4.0, MIT (with separately attributed third-party components)
https://github.com/jsawruk/finale-file-parser
Only the notation-reading and MusicXML-export modules are used. Its optional
inspection-report renderer (Verovio) is not needed or included in this app.
Its distribution license is retained in deps/. The upstream third-party notices,
including Mark Adler's blast.c/zlib notice, are retained separately in
LICENSES/finale-file-parser-NOTICES.md because the wheel omits that notice file.
Application mus_compat.py uses and adapts its MIT-licensed record decoders/text
section handling, adding structural layouts and active-frame validation.

https://lxml.de/

https://github.com/tiran/defusedxml

OpenSheetMusicDisplay 1.9.2, BSD-3-Clause
https://github.com/opensheetmusicdisplay/opensheetmusicdisplay
Bundled browser renderer. License retained in LICENSE-OSMD.txt.
Other bundled dependency notices remain in the renderer and wheel distributions.

All packaged wheels and runtime digests are recorded in BUILD-MANIFEST.json.
No customer documents or sample scores are included.

The source repository additionally includes a public test-only MUSX fixture from
openmusx/musxdom (MIT, Copyright (c) 2025 Robert G. Patterson).
Its commit, file hash and license are recorded in tests/fixtures/README.md and
LICENSES/musxdom-MIT.txt. Tests and fixtures are excluded from application ZIPs.
